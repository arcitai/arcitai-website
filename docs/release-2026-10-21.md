# Arc’IT website release — 21 October 2026

The owner approved publication of the redesigned website to `arcitai.com`.
This release presents data and business context as the foundation for useful
AI work, with two offers and the Factory engineering-method explainer.

## Included

- Approved blue/white brand mark, typography, light/dark themes and compact hero.
- The Open bridge Factory, reversible unfolding, five layers, independent
  Codex/Claude workers, module colours and sequential surface-copy transitions.
- Business Intelligence as the diagram module; Power BI for Factory retains
  its project name. Public project links keep their verified GitHub sources.
- A free 15-minute consultation linked directly to the supplied Notion Calendar
  booking page. Project inquiry copy focuses on ideas, stage and outcomes.
- Larger small text in the Factory and footer; Teams and Slack as channel
  examples. The Factory introduction omits “by Arc’IT”.
- Production excludes preview controls and the local OpenPaperDesign destination.

## Verification and scope

The release gates passed: lockfile installation, formatting, ESLint, 106 tests,
TypeScript, production build, dependency audit (zero vulnerabilities), and
Worker checks plus a dry-run bundle. The existing Worker contract is unchanged;
this release deploys only the static Pages site.

The built preview routes and entry assets were compared with served bytes.
Both production origins passed inquiry preflight; intentionally empty payloads
returned 400 with the correct origin. No real inquiry or appointment was created.
The Notion booking title, 15-minute duration and recurring availability were
checked separately. Calendar configuration is private operational state, not
website source.

Local rendered QA was unavailable after an earlier automatic localhost-browser
rejection. Source, test and HTTP evidence are distinct from visual proof. The
owner approved the visible preview. The final bounded Opus 5.5 Max review timed
out without a verdict. Post-deploy inspection must use the actual production
site, and its outcome is recorded in the owner’s delivery notes.
