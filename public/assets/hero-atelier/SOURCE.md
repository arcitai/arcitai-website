# Living Atelier

Original Arc’IT hero proposal generated on 21 October 2026 in Google Flow.

- Still: Nano Banana 2.1, one 16:9 output, displayed cost 0 credits.
- Motion: Omni 1.1 Flash, one 8-second 720p output, displayed cost 12 credits.
- The same original still was used for the first and last frames. The camera is
  fixed while the crane, workers and clouds move locally.
- Inspiration: [Elaya’s portfolio](https://elaya.design/) and
  [X profile](https://x.com/elayadesigns). A portfolio screenshot of the blue
  engraved robot landscape was supplied as a style reference. The reference
  artwork is not included in the website; the workshop composition is original.
- Delivery video: silent H.264, 1280 × 720, 24 fps, fast-start MP4.
  The source audio track was removed and the video compressed from 4.77 MB to
  1.97 MB. The JPEG poster is 1376 × 768.

This is a local review proposal, not the approved production hero. The asset is
editorial brand artwork and is excluded from the repository’s MIT code licence.
Prompts and generation evidence are retained in the task’s hero output folder.
