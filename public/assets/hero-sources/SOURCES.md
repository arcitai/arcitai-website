# Additional hero source marks · 22 October 2026

Illustrative sources and stores of business data for the local Arc’IT hero.
These are not partnership badges or a claim that an integration is installed.
All files are served locally. Logo paths and proportions are preserved; the
small surrounding tiles and their motion are original site artwork. Product
marks retain their owners’ rights. File URLs, modifications and SHA-256 hashes
are recorded in `SHA256SUMS.json`.

- **Shopify:** unmodified full-colour Shopping Bag SVG linked from the official
  [brand-assets page](https://www.shopify.com/brand-assets). The white S and
  two greens (#95BF47, #5E8E3E) are preserved. The shopping bag is the compact
  brandmark rather than a compressed wordmark.
- **Klaviyo:** unmodified 256px PNG icon linked from the current
  [Klaviyo homepage](https://www.klaviyo.com/), at
  https://www.klaviyo.com/icons/icon-256x256.png. Its flag silhouette matches
  the identity described in the [official brand announcement](https://www.klaviyo.com/newsroom/klaviyo-rebrand).
  Black and neutral context colours follow this actual asset.
- **Google Drive:** unmodified current 2026 vector, linked from the official
  [Drive product page](https://workspace.google.com/products/drive/):
  https://www.gstatic.com/images/branding/productlogos/drive_2026/v2/web/192px.svg.
  The downloaded vector retains all original gradient definitions.
- **Airtable**, **Dropbox**, **Snowflake:** CC0 Simple Icons renditions, paths
  unchanged, with catalogue colours #18BFFF, #0061FF and #29B5E8 respectively.
  [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) is the artwork
  source, not a claim of vendor-provided vectors. `SimpleIcons-LICENSE.txt`
  preserves the source licence. Catalogue references point to
  [Airtable](https://www.airtable.com/newsroom),
  [Dropbox](https://www.dropbox.com/branding) and
  [Snowflake](https://www.snowflake.com/brand-guidelines/).

Existing Slack, Teams, HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, GitHub, Azure, Fabric,
Databricks and Delta Lake artwork and provenance remain in `../factory-tools/`.
No existing Factory artwork or animation file is changed by this addition.

## Expanded business-source catalogue

Twenty-seven additional marks join the six local source assets above and six
existing Factory source marks, giving Blend a pool of 39 distinct sources.
Only 8–11 appear on desktop (6–7 on compact screens) at once. Selection spans
communication, documents, design, development, CRM, marketing, commerce,
finance, operations, work management and data stores, with at most two marks
from a category in one visit.

- **Gmail** and **Google Calendar:** unmodified full-colour SVGs from Google's
  product-logo host, at the exact source URLs recorded in the checksum manifest.
- **Figma:** unmodified full-colour `figma-original.svg` from
  [Devicon](https://github.com/devicons/devicon/tree/master/icons/figma).
  `Devicon-LICENSE.txt` preserves its MIT licence.
- The remaining 24 additions are [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons)
  vectors. Their paths and proportions are unchanged; only the root fill is
  set to the catalogue's brand colour. The manifest identifies the artwork
  source separately from its referenced vendor page. The existing CC0 licence
  file covers these vectors; product marks retain their owners' rights.
