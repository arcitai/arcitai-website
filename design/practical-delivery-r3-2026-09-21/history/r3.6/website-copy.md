## Start

### Project inquiry.

Describe the software, who uses it and the work you need help with. I’ll review the context and respond with a proposed next step.

### Do I need another platform?

Often we can work with the tools you already use, with clearer instructions, checks and review. I add new tools only when they solve a real need for your project.

### What do you check?

We agree what production-ready means for your use case, then review the code, data access, integrations and deployment. I fix relevant findings and check the changes again. Instructions alone don’t enforce permissions, and a review isn’t a guarantee that nothing can go wrong.

### Can you keep looking after it?

Yes. I can take care of agreed updates, fixes and ongoing development. We define the systems I’m responsible for, the review process and the support you need, including response times. You stay involved in business decisions and approvals.

### Prefer to build it yourself?

onlinesourdough offers a practical method, resources and hands-on guidance for doing the work yourself. With Arc’IT AI, I take care of the agreed technical delivery and ongoing work.
