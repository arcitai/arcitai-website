## Let’s talk

### Show me what you’ve got.

Tell me what it does, who uses it and where you’re unsure. We’ll work out whether I can help and what a useful first step looks like.

### Do I need another platform?

Often we can use what you already have — such as Codex or Cursor — with better instructions, skills, tests and review. I recommend additional software only where the work needs it.

### What do you check?

We agree what production-ready means for your use case, then review the code, data access, integrations and deployment. I fix relevant findings and check the changes again. Instructions alone don’t enforce permissions, and a review isn’t a guarantee that nothing can go wrong.

### Can you keep looking after it?

Yes. We agree what I’m responsible for, how changes are reviewed and what ongoing support includes. It can be a focused piece of work or a longer collaboration.

## Editorial decisions

- onlinesourdough is the method for working well with AI. The software factory and defense factory are two reusable AI-enabled systems, built from templates and methods, that Arc’IT sets up and looks after for the customer. They work through suitable existing tools; they are not a claim that Gustav has built a proprietary platform.
- The software factory supports ongoing development in the project; the defense factory organizes security review and improvement around that software. Human approval and enforced access controls remain necessary. The third offer explains the pair without reintroducing the large loop section.
- “Production-ready” is the intended outcome for the agreed use case, not an unconditional claim of security or readiness before testing. Keep promising demo, MVP and prototype as recognizable starting points rather than a new branded offer name.
- A working prototype is a useful starting point. Do not imply that every AI-built tool is unsafe or that Arc’IT can remove all technical debt.
- Keep the value practical: inspect, prioritise, fix, test and establish responsibility. No invented customer results, prices, certifications or vendor relationship.
- R1, R2 and their browser snapshots were recovered. The exact additional Chrome variation the owner remembered was not identified in the inspected files/tasks. This is a new revision using the recovered text and the latest brief, not a claimed recovery of an unseen design.
