# Arc’IT AI — Open landscape / R4

Status: composition proposal for owner review in OpenPencil. The existing
React site, localhost build, production and R3 designs remain unchanged.

## Brief

Give the accepted service landing page more presence, chiefly in the navigation,
hero and actions. Keep the practical copy, three offers (two compact, one full
width), original Cut Six mark, landscape scenes, player and motion language.
This is a composition study, not a new product or a rewrite of the offer.

## One idea

A bright editorial opening beside an unobscured landscape. The message has its
own paper surface; the scene no longer needs a dark wash to carry body copy.
An oversized, exact white Cut Six mark gives the scene a graphic focal point.
Hierarchy comes from asymmetric proportions, scale and a decisive primary CTA.

## Composition

- Desktop: 1440px canvas, 80px outside margin, 1280px opening. Text-only
  wordmark, an opaque compact charcoal navigation capsule (Offers / Process /
  Scope / Start), and the Resources-style theme control. No glass treatment.
- Headline: large Geist Sans, left aligned, same promise: “From prototype to
  production-ready software.” The wordmark and selected section titles retain
  Geist Pixel. This shifts emphasis without replacing the identity.
- Hero: text and actions on paper, daylight scene in a tall rounded panel to
  the right. The mark stays white, never green. No broad image tint. One small
  solid caption surface holds the existing business/security line.
- CTA: charcoal rounded control, inset sage arrow disk, clear focus outline in
  implementation. The secondary action is a quiet text/link control; both keep
  their existing destinations. Mobile keeps the primary action above the art.
- Introduction: two-part composition, with existing explanation at left and
  the current rounded 16:9 scene player at right. Hover/focus play behavior is
  unchanged; the shown Play state is an interaction study, not a recorded VSL.
- Offers: same two-plus-one arrangement, existing abstract motifs and copy.
  Thin rules and larger headings give rhythm without a wall of cards. The full
  factory section remains a quiet, distinct surface with the accepted pipe.
- Inquiry and FAQs remain the current accepted R3.9 layout, including all four
  answers initially open. Footer still shares the selected scene.

## Color, type and motion

Paper #f8f2e8, near-black #18211d, muted #615f56, green #236b59 and sage #b9d9c8.
Dark reverses the paper/text roles, not the logo rule; night imagery is not
covered by an extra green wash. Use natural daylight color as the largest accent.
Desktop hero 76px Sans / 1.02, body 18–20px / 1.45; mobile hero 42px / 1.04,
body 17px. Do not shrink explanatory copy to fit a composition.

Retain the actual logo assembly, offer studies and reversible factory flow.
The larger mark can use the existing small, slow displacement; no continuous
spinning or mascot behavior. The landscape uses the received scene loop.
Reduced motion, data saving, visibility pause, theme/scene selection and real
player controls keep their current behavior when/if implemented.

## Selected companion and limitations

OpenPencil contains an editable desktop composition through the factory offer,
a mobile composition through the offers, and a dark hero study. The unchanged
inquiry/footer are not redrawn. A separate motion-state frame documents the
existing animation roles. Native boards are static: they do not prove playback,
hover, responsive reflow or form submission. Working interactions remain in the
unchanged localhost implementation.

Preserve the R3.9 implementation until the owner accepts this direction. Review
the hero first, then the transition into the existing content. This is a proposed
alternative, not the new operative production design.
