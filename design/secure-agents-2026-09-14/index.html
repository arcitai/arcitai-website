LET’S TALK

Tell me what you’d like to improve.

You might be exploring a first workflow, connecting AI to company data, or looking for someone to take care of a setup you already have.

Tell me a little about the work and what you need. I’ll help you work out whether Arc’IT is a fit and what a sensible first step could be.

Does this fit our business?

The strongest fit is work where agents connect to company data or processes other people rely on. That often means mid-size and larger teams. A smaller business can have the same need, and we can start with one workflow.

Do we need a new platform?

We start with the tools you have. I’ll recommend changing them when there’s a clear reason, rather than making a new platform the starting point.

What does “secure” mean here?

It means designing around the data and actions involved, putting practical limits in place, testing them, and maintaining the agreed setup. Risk doesn’t disappear. A certification, independent audit or round-the-clock incident response would need its own specialist scope.
