## Two systems / One delivery process

### From agreed work to reviewed changes.

I put the workflow in place and look after the parts we agree I’ll run. You get a clear view of what’s moving, what’s been checked, and what needs your attention. The setup can use the tools that fit your business.

**AI Software Factory**

Turn agreed tasks into focused changes, tests and reviewable pull requests. Keep the work tied to the result the business needs.

**AI Agent Defense Factory**

Review the system and its boundaries, investigate findings, and verify focused fixes. Keep security checks connected to the work as it changes.

Interactive **Software loop:** Triage → Specify → Build → Review → Human approval.

Interactive **Defense loop:** Review → Validate → Fix → Verify → Human approval.

The final step in both views: **A person decides what goes live.** Review the change and its evidence through the agreed release process. Approval, rollback and ongoing responsibility are explicit. Verification doesn’t automatically merge or deploy anything.

Both systems work on the same change. Missing or inconclusive checks stay visible; neither system approves its own release. Access limits and a way to stop or recover belong in the setup, too.

## Inquiry

LET’S TALK

### What would you like to take off your plate?

Tell me about the work, what you already have and where you’re stuck. I’ll help you work out whether Arc’IT is a fit and what a sensible first step could be.

**Can you take over something we already have?**

Yes, that can be the starting point. I first review the setup and its dependencies, then we agree what needs work and what I’ll be responsible for. A useful prototype is worth building on when the foundations make sense.

**What does “secure” mean here?**

Clear access limits, appropriate approvals, tests for relevant failures and a way to pause or recover. I build these into the setup and maintain the agreed parts. Risk doesn’t disappear, and an independent audit or round-the-clock incident response needs its own specialist scope.

**Do the factories need another platform?**

They’re workflows I help you set up around your systems and delivery process. We choose the agent tools and hosting to fit your requirements. The useful part is a repeatable process, a clear overview and someone responsible for keeping it working.

**What if I want to build it myself?**

That’s where [onlinesourdough](https://onlinesourdough.com/) fits. Use the resources yourself or work through the decisions with me. The same business-first thinking and sensible safeguards apply; the difference is who takes care of the delivery.
