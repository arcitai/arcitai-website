## Start with the work you want to improve.

Hey, I’m Gustav. I work across business and software architecture. I’ll help you decide where AI is useful, make the setup work with your team, and take responsibility for the parts we agree I’ll run.

Sometimes that means building. Sometimes it means simplifying the process or making better use of what you already have.

#### Example treatment

Label: **ILLUSTRATIVE WORKFLOW**

### An agent that helps prepare quotes.

Give it the approved pricing and a selected client brief. Let it prepare a draft for a colleague to review.

**Approved inputs → Draft quote → Human approval**

**What it can read**

The selected brief and approved pricing.

**What stays with a person**

Approving and sending the quote. Changing source prices.

**When required pricing is missing**

The workflow returns the draft for clarification.

I’d enforce those limits in the permissions and workflow, then test whether they hold. Instructions alone aren’t enough.

_An example of the approach, not a customer case._

### What I take care of

Eyebrow: **WHAT I TAKE CARE OF**

## From the first decision to the day-to-day work.

You bring the work you’d like to improve. I handle the technical decisions and delivery, with your team involved where their knowledge or approval is needed.

#### 01 / Understand

**Choose one worthwhile change.**

We map the work, choose the result worth aiming for, and agree where the agent should help and where a person stays in charge. We define how we’ll judge whether the change is worth the effort.

Row note: **OUTCOME / SCOPE / RESPONSIBILITY**

#### 02 / Set up

**Build around your business.**

I configure the tools, context and connections the work needs. That can include software and integrations, with access limited to the data and actions the task requires.

Row note: **TOOLS / DATA / PERMISSIONS**

#### 03 / Test

**Test the work and its boundaries.**

I review the setup and relevant code, test the important failure paths, fix what we find, and check the changes again. That includes misleading input and attempts to use data or actions outside the agreed scope.

Row note: **REVIEW / FIX / RETEST**

#### 04 / Run

**Keep it working as things change.**

I look after the agreed setup, including maintenance and reviews when the tools, access or workflows change. We define what I own, what your team owns, and how to pause or recover the system.

Row note: **MAINTENANCE / OWNERSHIP / RECOVERY**

### Inquiry

Eyebrow: **LET’S TALK**

## Tell me what you’d like to improve.

You might be exploring a first workflow, connecting AI to company data, or looking for someone to take care of a setup you already have.

Tell me a little about the work and what you need. I’ll help you work out whether Arc’IT is a fit and what a sensible first step could be.

#### Practical questions

**Does this fit our business?**

The strongest fit is work where agents connect to company data or processes other people rely on. That often means mid-size and larger teams. A smaller business can have the same need, and we can start with one workflow.

**Do we need a new platform?**

We start with the tools you have. I’ll recommend changing them when there’s a clear reason, rather than making a new platform the starting point.

**What does “secure” mean here?**

It means designing around the data and actions involved, putting practical limits in place, testing them, and maintaining the agreed setup. Risk doesn’t disappear. A certification, independent audit or round-the-clock incident response would need its own specialist scope.

## Implementation notes, not page copy

- Recommend A. B and C are alternatives, not additional sections or a proposed A/B experiment.
- Keep the existing scenic, warm identity. Avoid a shield/lock visual system, invented security scores, customer logos and claims such as “enterprise-grade”, “fully secure” or “OpenAI partner”.
- Use the example in the current founder-explanation area until a reviewed film exists. No fake play control, duration or chapter timestamps. A future VSL can explain this same promise; it is outside this iteration.
- Keep the public Offers menu to the existing two entries. Agent Defense Factory is an internal delivery hypothesis, not a third offer. No additional lead-magnet CTA until the asset and signup route exist and are approved.
- This proposed form is intentionally lighter. A future implementation must reconcile `src/site-data.ts`, the inquiry types, Worker validation, tests and Notion schema. Do not merely relabel or silently discard required revenue/role/size fields in the current API.
- Draft status does not establish operational readiness. Confirm deliverable scope, capability, specialist escalation, capacity, data terms and ongoing commitments before accepting engagements on this basis. No price, SLA, certification or availability is invented here.
